# Rap

So at times [[my ego]] would be like,

"How come all these rappers are rich and some of them aren't even trying hard?" That used to be my mindset, probably when I was 22, about 4 years ago. I've always been a rap fan, and I've always admired [[Kendrick Lamar]], West Coast rap, [[Tyler the Creator]], [[Kanye West]]. Nothing on those guys, it's more like [[6ix9ine]] and [[Lil Pump]], so I'd be like, "If they can do it, I can do it in my own industry."

Now that I'm older and time has passed, I'm listening to the podcast [[Dissect]], which breaks down the lyrics and instrumentals of music in general. The season I'm listening to is [[Kendrick Lamar]]'s [[Mr. Morale and the Big Steppers]]. I realize rap ***is about capturing moments***, and if you do it well enough, songs are either evergreen or they capture a moment so well that other brands, people, or movies use them to bring back that moment.

A great example is the song [[Squabble Up]]. In 2025, the [[Dodgers]] won the [[World Series]] again and were back-to-back champions. Right after they won, [[Nike]] released a commercial with some credits, with the song [[Squabble Up]]. Then I'm thinking, "Wait, Kendrick didn't just make music, didn't just make money off of releasing the album and doing the concerts. He also got for sure a big fat check from Nike, which is like pretty fucking cool."

Connecting it back to how I should live my life, it's like create things that capture moments and just put them out there. **Make something really good, put it out there,** and keep working hard. You can be strategic, like [[Vince Staples]], when he released [[Big Fish]]. He was like, "I'm trying to make songs that can be used on movies and on commercials because those are the big checks," and yes, that makes total fucking sense. I don't know, I just thought it was a really cool thing to look at.

And this thought was triggered from me watching this What's the Best Song on [[GNX]]? How they were talking about how [[Luther]] is probably [[Kendrick Lamar]]'s biggest song and how it could be played in so many scenarios, like in weddings, like in love movies, like not just that. I'm sure there are way more scenarios but they were just talking about how it was bigger, it's probably bigger than [[Not Like Us]].
