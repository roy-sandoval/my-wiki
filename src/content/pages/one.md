I have healed. Yet those around me still feel pain. My mother is in her process. My sister has her pain and is in her process. My father has his pain and is in his process. 

I am blessed enough to have my parents around, so I will talk to them to set them free. It is not my responsbility, it is not my power, it is simply my love.

I don't know how much guilt they carry. Probably some probably none. What is clear is the pain.

I used to wonder why my father would never talk about his father. Now I realize how painful it could be to confront reality. There's two possibilities. I wish it's the simpler one. The one that sets him free. If it's the other one there's still some liberation.

One door opens a world where he learns he has always been wanted. 
The other opens a world where his mother was taken advantage of.

Either way it sets her free.

It's not personal.

It's breaking with our pain.
